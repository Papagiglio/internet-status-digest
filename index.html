Mastercard (cardholder benefit-program data) — Financial services / payments; USA / Purchase, New York (HQ); Threat actor: n0n; Reported: Nov 19; Status: Confirmed (third-party provider). Sonoco — Manufacturing (packaging); USA / Hartsville, South Carolina (HQ, per company; not stated in source); Threat actor: Helix; Reported: Nov 17; Status: Claimed (unverified).
