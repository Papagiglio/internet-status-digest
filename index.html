Daily Internet & Cloud Health Digest, Nov 14, 2026 (noon CT)

Source: Scout run 2026-11-14-1040.md, plus a live search for the last few hours and direct checks of Cleo, OCI and Azure.

1. Cyber incidents

University of Texas at Arlington

  • Industry: Education (higher ed)
  • Country / Location: United States, Arlington, Texas
  • Threat actor: Qilin ransomware (attributed)
  • Date reported: Nov 13–14, 2026
  • Status: Confirmed
  • Summary: UT Arlington confirmed that personal data was stolen in an attack attributed to Qilin. Some of the data includes Social Security numbers.
  • Supply chain / third-party risk: Students, staff and research partners may have exposed identity data, which could lead to downstream phishing and fraud.
  • Source: UXC News

UnrealIRCd (CVE-2026-114713), actively exploited

  • Industry: Technology provider (open-source software)
  • Country / Location: Global (affected software, no single location stated)
  • Threat actor: Unknown
  • Date reported: Nov 13, 2026
  • Status: Confirmed (CISA says it is exploited)
  • Summary: CISA added this flaw to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on Nov 13. Federal agencies must patch it.
  • Supply chain / third-party risk: Any organization running self-hosted IRC servers is exposed, including some embedded and hosting setups.
  • Source: CISA alert
