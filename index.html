1. Cyber incidents

U.S. Air Force Cloud One breach

  • Industry: Government / Defense
  • Country / Location: United States (Department of the Air Force)
  • Threat Actor: Unknown
  • Date Reported: Nov 27
  • Status: Investigating
  • Summary: Reuters sources say hackers breached the Air Force's flagship Cloud One platform, found within the past month during an upgrade, and it has disrupted the platform. Scope, attacker and any data theft are unconfirmed, and the Air Force declined to comment.
  • Supply chain / third-party risk: Cloud One hosts sensitive tactical, personnel and logistics data, so defense contractors and connected systems may be exposed.
  • Source: CNBC/Reuters

UK Department for Work and Pensions (DWP)

  • Industry: Government
  • Country / Location: United Kingdom
  • Threat Actor: Unknown
  • Date Reported: Nov 27
  • Status: Investigating
  • Summary: DWP took some online services offline as a precaution after a cyber incident and is working with the NCSC. Regular benefit and pension payments are not affected.
  • Supply chain / third-party risk: Contractors and agencies that connect to DWP online services may see disruption; scope not stated.
  • Source: GOV.UK, BBC

Manchester Airports Group (MAG)

  • Industry: Logistics & Transportation (airports)
  • Country / Location: United Kingdom (Manchester, Stansted, East Midlands)
  • Threat Actor: Unknown
  • Date Reported: Nov 27
  • Status: Confirmed
  • Summary: MAG says an unauthorized third party accessed data on about 8.7 million customers, mostly email addresses, linked to Wi-Fi sign-ups, parking, lounges and fast-track. No payment data was affected and flight operations were not impacted.
  • Supply chain / third-party risk: Customer contact data could fuel phishing against travelers; the access path has not been detailed.
  • Source: İLKHA

Octopus Cards Limited

  • Industry: Payments / Transportation
  • Country / Location: Hong Kong
  • Threat Actor: Unknown
  • Date Reported: Nov 27
  • Status: Confirmed
  • Summary: Octopus detected abnormal activity on Nov 27 and contained it. About 7,500 customers (names, photos, some auto-top-up bank code and amount) and about 22,000 merchants' contact details were affected. Core services still operate and no financial loss has been found.
  • Supply chain / third-party risk: Merchant contact data could be used for targeted phishing.
  • Source: Octopus press release

SmartPLAY (LCSD contractor system)

  • Industry: Government services / Technology provider
  • Country / Location: Hong Kong
  • Threat Actor: Unknown
  • Date Reported: Nov 26–27
  • Status: Confirmed
  • Summary: Hong Kong's LCSD confirmed its SmartPLAY booking system was illegally accessed, involving personal data linked to about 90,000 Leisure Link accounts. The contractor stopped the hack and the department is investigating.
  • Supply chain / third-party risk: The flaw sits in a contractor-run system, so other systems from the same vendor may be affected.
  • Source: news.gov.hk

Aptean (ERP software vendor)

  • Industry: Technology Provider (ERP for manufacturers, distributors, food)
  • Country / Location: United States, Alpharetta, Georgia
  • Threat Actor: The Gentlemen
  • Date Reported: Nov 26
  • Status: Claimed
  • Summary: The Gentlemen listed Aptean on its leak site, claiming many terabytes of data including client files. Aptean has made no statement and the claim is unverified.
  • Supply chain / third-party risk: High, since Aptean's software serves many manufacturing and food customers.
  • Source: BreachNews

Restaurant Brands International (Burger King, Tim Hortons, Popeyes)

  • Industry: Food & Retail
  • Country / Location: Canada, Toronto (not stated in source; HQ per public record; verify)
  • Threat Actor: Inception
  • Date Reported: Nov 26
  • Status: Claimed
  • Summary: Inception claims about 800,000 files (support tickets, loyalty records, partial payment details, OneDrive and Slack exports) and gave a 48-hour deadline. No samples have been shown.
  • Supply chain / third-party risk: Franchisee and customer data may be exposed; unverified.
  • Source: Cybernews

USA Today (Gannett)

  • Industry: Media
  • Country / Location: United States, New York
  • Threat Actor: n0n ransomware group
  • Date Reported: Nov 26
  • Status: Claimed
  • Summary: n0n claims it stole about 166 million subscriber identity records. The company has not yet commented.

  • Source: Cybernews
