1. Cyber incidents

FBI — National Crime Information Center (NCIC) intrusion

  • Industry: Government / law enforcement
  • Country / Location: United States, Washington, DC
  • Threat Actor: Unknown (no link to ShinyHunters' September FBI job-portal breach reported)
  • Date Reported: Nov 21, 2026
  • Status: Investigating
  • Summary: CNN, NBC and AP report the FBI is investigating an apparent hack of the NCIC, the nationwide criminal-justice database. It is unclear how the intrusion happened or whether records were taken. Officials say the recent wave of US outages is not linked to criminal activity.
  • Supply chain / third-party risk: Police and agencies nationwide query NCIC, so access or integrity problems could affect law-enforcement and background-check workflows.
  • Sources: NBC News, BleepingComputer, AP

Rokkatei (六花亭) online shop — payment-page tampering

  • Industry: Retail / Food (confectionery)
  • Country / Location: Japan, Hokkaido
  • Threat Actor: Unknown
  • Date Reported: Nov 18–21, 2026
  • Status: Confirmed
  • Summary: Rokkatei says its online shop payment page was tampered with and confirmed one fraudulent card charge of about ¥50,000 on Nov 20. About 8,000 transactions may be affected, and names, addresses, phones and emails may be exposed.
  • Supply chain / third-party risk: Customer card data exposure; the payment-page compromise points to a web-skimming risk for e-commerce platforms.
  • Sources: Rokkatei notice
