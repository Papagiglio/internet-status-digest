1. Cyber incidents

US Treasury FinCEN (federal)

  • Industry: Government / Financial intelligence
  • Country / Location: United States, Washington, DC
  • Threat Actor: Unknown
  • Date Reported: Nov 24–25
  • Status: Investigating
  • Brief Summary: FinCEN says unauthorized access hit a limited subset of its systems and it took some offline; the BSA E-Filing site is up but some services are unavailable. It granted temporary filing-deadline relief on Nov 24 and has not said whether data was taken.
  • Potential Supply Chain/Third-Party Risk Impact: Banks and financial institutions that file SARs/CTRs via BSA E-Filing face delays and deadline changes.
  • Source: SecurityWeek

CIA / ODNI / NGA open-source intelligence system (federal)

  • Industry: Government / Intelligence
  • Country / Location: United States
  • Threat Actor: Unknown; early indications point to the AI hacking tool "Artex" and a possible China link (unconfirmed)
  • Date Reported: Nov 24
  • Status: Investigating (reportedly resolved, no disruption to critical capabilities)
  • Brief Summary: Hackers breached an unclassified open-source-intelligence system used by the CIA, ODNI and NGA, per the New York Times and AP. No classified information was reportedly exposed.
  • Potential Supply Chain/Third-Party Risk Impact: Shared system across several agencies; contractors using it may be affected.
  • Sources: Washington Post, Nextgov

University Hospital of Guadeloupe (CHU)

  • Industry: Healthcare
  • Country / Location: France (Guadeloupe, Caribbean)
  • Threat Actor: Unknown (no ransom demand received)
  • Date Reported: Nov 24–25 (systems down since Nov 21 evening)
  • Status: Investigating / Ongoing
  • Brief Summary: A suspected ransomware attack encrypted databases, forcing surgeries and imaging to be canceled through at least Friday and radiation therapy to be suspended. ANSSI is investigating whether access came through a compromised IT supplier; recovery is expected to take days.
  • Potential Supply Chain/Third-Party Risk Impact: Possible compromise via an unnamed IT supplier, which could affect other customers.
  • Source: The Record

Radware

  • Industry: Cybersecurity / DDoS & WAF vendor
  • Country / Location: Israel (Tel Aviv HQ); customers global
  • Threat Actor: Unknown
  • Date Reported: Nov 24 (access Nov 19)
  • Status: Investigating
  • Brief Summary: An unauthenticated internal Cloud WAF management API exposed customer contact details and, for some customers, log-export credentials (AWS S3, Azure Blob, SFTP). Radware says traffic inspection was not affected, but some customers saw AWS alerts for unfamiliar IPs.
  • Potential Supply Chain/Third-Party Risk Impact: Customers should rotate exposed keys and review storage logs since Nov 19.
  • Source: BleepingComputer

Turnitin

  • Industry: Education technology
  • Country / Location: United States (HQ not stated in source); users in 185 countries
  • Threat Actor: Unknown
  • Date Reported: Nov 24
  • Status: Investigating
  • Brief Summary: Turnitin detected unauthorized activity on customer-facing systems and notified law enforcement and regulators. It says systems are operational and don't hold government IDs or institutional financial data; scope is undisclosed.
  • Potential Supply Chain/Third-Party Risk Impact: Used by about 16,000 institutions, so schools and universities are potentially exposed.
  • Sources: BleepingComputer, SecurityWeek

Meridian Capital Group

  • Industry: Finance / Commercial real estate lending
  • Country / Location: Not stated
  • Threat Actor: Qilin
  • Date Reported: Nov 25
  • Status: Confirmed
  • Brief Summary: Meridian confirmed a breach, activated its response plan and is working with law enforcement. Qilin could expose financial documents such as rent rolls and operating reports.
  • Potential Supply Chain/Third-Party Risk Impact: Clients and lending counterparties' documents may be exposed.
  • Source: Cyware
