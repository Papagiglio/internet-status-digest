1. Cyber incidents

Osaki Electric Co., Ltd. — Manufacturing (smart meters); Japan / Tokyo; Threat actor: Unknown (not claimed); Reported: Nov 18; Status: Confirmed. Osaki disclosed to the Tokyo Stock Exchange a ransomware/extortion attack that halted production. No group has claimed it yet. Supply chain risk: production halt can delay meter deliveries to utilities and downstream customers. Sources: Ransomnews, ransomware.live press

NVIDIA (claimed victim) — Technology / semiconductors; USA / Santa Clara, California (HQ); Threat actor: The Gentlemen; Reported: Nov 18; Status: Claimed (unverified). The Gentlemen listed Nvidia on its leak site with a ~234-hour (<10 days) countdown but showed no evidence of stolen data. Nvidia has not confirmed a breach. Supply chain risk: large B2B/partner footprint; a real leak could expose partner or customer data. Source: Cybernews

Equinor — Energy; Norway / Stavanger (HQ); Threat actor: Unknown; Reported: Nov 17; Status: Investigating. Equinor detected a cyberattack on some IT systems. No data theft or production impact reported so far. Supply chain risk: low so far; watch for vendor/partner IT exposure. Source: Equinor

Oracle Java "Java4Shell" (CVE-2026-114938) — Technology (software); Global; Threat actor: Unknown; Reported: Nov 17; Status: Investigating / patch urgently. CISA urged immediate patching of this critical unauthenticated RCE in the Java x86-64 HotSpot Base64 decoder. Aptible and Akamai have posted customer advisories. Supply chain risk: Java is embedded in many B2B/EDI and enterprise stacks; inventory and patch. Sources: CISA, Rewindex
