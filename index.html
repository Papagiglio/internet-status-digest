1. Cyber incidents

Mitel MiCollab zero-days (CVE-2026-133275/133303/133311/133323/133324) exploited

  • Industry: Technology Provider (unified communications)
  • Country / Location: Global (vendor HQ not stated in source)
  • Threat Actor: Unknown
  • Date Reported: Nov 25–26
  • Status: Confirmed (active exploitation; added to CISA KEV Nov 25; federal agencies given 3 days to fix four of them)
  • Summary: Five unauthenticated MiCollab flaws, including a CVSS 10 command injection, were exploited before Mitel's Nov 16 advisory in what looks like a data-theft/extortion campaign. No ransomware deployment was confirmed.
  • Supply chain / third-party risk: Organizations using MiCollab for voice and collaboration, and their partners, may have exposed call and contact data; patch and review for compromise.
  • Sources: SecurityWeek, CISA KEV additions

Drupal Core RCE (CVE-2026-132165) exploited

  • Industry: Technology Provider (CMS)
  • Country / Location: Global
  • Threat Actor: Unknown
  • Date Reported: Nov 26 (exploitation since Nov 19)
  • Status: Confirmed, ongoing
  • Summary: Attackers are exploiting a critical Drupal Core remote code execution flaw to hijack sites; upgrading or blocking /core/install.php is the mitigation.
  • Supply chain / third-party risk: Many government, retail and media sites and agencies' hosted web properties run Drupal.
  • Source: BleepingComputer
