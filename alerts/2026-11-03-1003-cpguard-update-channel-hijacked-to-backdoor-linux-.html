cPGuard update channel hijacked to backdoor Linux servers

An attacker used stolen credentials to push a malicious script through cPGuard's update system on Nov 2 (versions 5.88.12 to 5.88.19), backdooring customers' Linux servers and stealing password hashes and other sensitive files; fixed in 5.88.25, but affected servers need new passwords and keys.
