Digest for Nov 11, 2026

Daily internet & cloud health digest, Nov 11, 2026 (noon CT) Source: Scout run 2026-11-11-1030.md, plus direct checks of Cleo, OCI and Azure.

1. Cyber incidents

  • CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities additions, Nov 10 (Industry: Technology providers / enterprise software; Country / Location: United States, CISA, Washington, DC; vendors include Cisco, Barracuda and Microsoft; Actor: Unknown; Reported Nov 10; Status: Confirmed, actively exploited). CISA added six flaws: Cisco BroadWorks, a Barracuda ESG auth bypass (federal fix due Nov 13), an Outlook zero-day, a Chromium zero-day and Openwave Webtop. Supply chain risk: email gateways, telecom platforms and mail clients sit inside many B2B environments, so unpatched copies expose partners. CISA
  • Maersk (LF Logistics legacy system) (Industry: Logistics & Transportation; Country / Location: Denmark, Copenhagen (Maersk HQ); Actor: a ransomware gang claims 16,000 driver passwords, name not stated; Reported Nov 11; Status: Confirmed data theft, contained). Maersk disclosed data theft from a legacy system inherited through LF Logistics, with no shipping disruption. Supply chain risk: driver credentials and logistics partner data could be reused against carriers and customers. Cybernews
  • Sega (via newsletter vendor) (Industry: Entertainment / technology; Country / Location: Japan, Tokyo (Sega HQ); Actor: Unknown; Reported Nov 11; Status: Confirmed). A third-party newsletter vendor breach exposed 2.2 million subscriber email addresses. Supply chain risk: a single marketing vendor exposed a whole customer list, and the addresses can feed phishing. IT Pro

2. Internet, network, cloud and EDI

  • Cleo (Global), OCI (Normal Performance) and Azure (no active events) are clear. AWS, Google Cloud (last incident resolved Nov 5) and Cloudflare (scheduled maintenance only) had no platform outage, and no other EDI VAN issues turned up. Resolved. Cleo Status

3. Status changes

  • Chrome zero-day CVE-2026-125350 (Global): earlier "patched, not in KEV" is now Chromium zero-day on CISA's KEV list, still patched. CISA
  • Vanderbilt University BYOD ransomware (United States, Nashville, Tennessee): still ongoing, but core services such as email are back while other systems stay restricted; the gang claims 1M+ SSNs. Inside Higher Ed
