1. Cyber incidents

U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) — Farm Production and Conservation systems — Government (agriculture); USA / Washington, DC (HQ), nationwide FSA/NRCS offices; Threat Actor: Unknown; Reported: Nov 19 (internal notice Nov 16); Status: Investigating (ongoing outages). USDA's cybersecurity operations center "uncovered a security incident," forcing resets of all database passwords and applications, which has caused multi-day outages of tools for farm loans, payments and crop insurance; some staff also had VPN trouble Thursday. It is unclear who is responsible or whether data was taken; USDA has not publicly confirmed a breach, and officials have described the outages as updates/maintenance. Supply chain risk: farmers, lenders and crop-insurance partners depend on these payment and loan tools; delays in federal payments and possible exposure of producer data. Sources: Nextgov/FCW, DTN, Cybernews

SBC Medical Group Holdings — Healthcare services; USA / California (HQ), patients in Japan; Threat Actor: Unknown; Reported: Nov 20; Status: Confirmed (remediated, investigation continuing). SEC filing discloses hackers accessed a managed online platform and stole data on over 2 million people tied to Japanese clinics (names, contact details, dates of birth, some treatment info, 14 credit cards, passwords). A separate incident at the Tribeau reservation service exposed about 19,000 people. Supply chain risk: third-party reservation platform (Tribeau) and clinic partners affected; credential reuse risk from stolen passwords. Source: SecurityWeek
