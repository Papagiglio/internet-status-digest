1. Cyber incidents

  • Visa (via Asia-Pacific third-party provider) and JPMorgan Chase — Industry: Financial Services / Payments; Country / Location: Visa: USA, San Francisco Bay Area (HQ), provider region Asia-Pacific; JPMorgan: USA, New York (HQ); Threat Actor: n0n ransomware gang; Date Reported: Nov 17; Status: Claimed (Visa confirmed a third-party incident; JPMorgan denies cyber link). Summary: n0n listed both firms with a 48-hour leak deadline. Visa says an Asia-Pacific provider was hit, not Visa systems or payment data. JPMorgan says its Nov 16 outage was a software bug, with no evidence of a cyberattack. Supply chain risk: unnamed Visa service provider could expose other payments clients. Sources: Cybernews
  • Infoblox — Industry: Technology Provider (DNS/DHCP/IPAM); Country / Location: USA (HQ Santa Clara, CA); Threat Actor: Unknown; Date Reported: Nov 17; Status: Confirmed / Investigating. Summary: Attackers used credentials of a third-party data-migration vendor to reach Infoblox's SharePoint. Reportedly stolen: employee data (incl. SSNs, direct-deposit details) and some customers' NIOS backups (DNS/DHCP configs, Grid databases). Infoblox says its products, cloud services and build pipeline were not involved. Supply chain risk: customers' network configuration snapshots could aid targeted attacks on their DNS/DHCP infrastructure. Sources: TechRadar
  • Uber (via AI provider Fireworks) — Industry: Transportation / Technology; Country / Location: USA (HQ San Francisco, CA); Threat Actor: Unknown financially motivated group (LLMjacking-linked, per Fireworks); Date Reported: Nov 17 (notices sent Nov 10); Status: Confirmed. Summary: Uber told 149,346 drivers their names, driver's license numbers, dates of birth and addresses were accessed through Uber's account at Fireworks. Uber cut its data connections to the provider. Supply chain risk: shows downstream exposure from AI/data service providers; the same Fireworks incident affected the Cursor/Claude Code extension earlier. Sources: BleepingComputer
  • Debenhams — Industry: Retail / E-commerce; Country / Location: United Kingdom; Threat Actor: n0n; Date Reported: Nov 16–17; Status: Claimed (data leaked). Summary: n0n published storefront data, including 15,708 customer emails and 8,530 password-recovery codes. Attack estimated Nov 9. Supply chain risk: account-takeover and phishing exposure for customers; e-commerce platform vendor involvement not stated. Sources: QPulse
