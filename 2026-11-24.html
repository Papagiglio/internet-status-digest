1. Cyber incidents

Keio Corp (Japan)

  • Industry: Transportation / Retail / Education conglomerate
  • Country / Location: Japan, Tokyo
  • Threat actor: Unknown
  • Date reported: Nov 24 (attack Sep 26)
  • Status: Confirmed / Investigating
  • Summary: Keio now says up to 11.08M personal records, including passport copies, may be exposed in the September ransomware attack.
  • Supply chain / third-party risk: Large travel, retail and transit customer base; passport data raises identity-fraud risk.

LaLiga (Spain, Madrid)

  • Industry: Sports / Media
  • Country / Location: Spain, Madrid
  • Threat actor: Unknown
  • Date reported: Nov 24
  • Status: Confirmed
  • Summary: LaLiga confirmed a cyberattack on its fan-services platform that exposed fans' personal data, and several clubs (e.g. CD Leganés) have acknowledged being affected.

Gatwick Airport, third-party payment system (United Kingdom)

  • Industry: Logistics & Transportation (aviation)
  • Country / Location: United Kingdom, Gatwick, West Sussex
  • Threat actor: Unknown
  • Date reported: Nov 24
  • Status: Confirmed / Investigating
  • Summary: A breach of a third-party drop-off charge payment system exposed customer login credentials, emails, home addresses, registered cars and in some cases card details.
  • Supply chain / third-party risk: Vendor-side breach; other airports using the same provider may be affected (not stated).
  • Source: BBC

European Commission, EU Academy (EU)
