1. Cyber incidents

WordPress sites (versions 4.7 through 7.1.3)

  • Industry: Technology providers (web platform)
  • Threat actor: Unknown (mass automated scanning)
  • Date reported: Nov 1, 2026
  • Status: Investigating (exploitation attempts, no confirmed compromises yet)
  • Summary: Wordfence says attackers are hammering wp-content/upgrade installer paths to hit the few-second window during full-package WordPress updates and get remote code execution through CVE-2026-119977, with roughly 58,700 to 63,000 blocked attempts in 24 hours. The fix is WordPress 7.1.4 (released Oct 28) or its branch backports.
  • Supply chain / third-party risk: Medium. Lots of vendor, supplier and customer-facing sites run WordPress, so any that haven't updated are exposed, and a hijacked site can be used to serve malware or phish partners.
  • Source: The Hacker News

b14g (Bitcoin staking protocol on the Core network)

  • Industry: Fintech / crypto
  • Threat actor: Unknown
  • Date reported: Oct 31–Nov 1, 2026
  • Status: Confirmed (investigating)
  • Summary: About 97 million staked CORE tokens were withdrawn without authorization at 4:29 AM CDT Oct 31 and sent to attacker addresses. b14g paused the affected contracts, exchanges froze about 9 million tokens, Core DAO says the Core network itself wasn't affected, and no post-mortem is out yet.
  • Supply chain / third-party risk: Low outside crypto.
  • Source: The Crypto Times

Gmail and YouTube users (abuse of real Google security alerts)

  • Industry: Technology providers / consumers
  • Threat actor: Unknown (scammers)
  • Date reported: Oct 31, 2026
  • Status: Ongoing
  • Summary: Scammers put fake invoice text, like a $399 McAfee charge, into Google app-password names on throwaway accounts, so Google sends genuine, authenticated security alerts that they forward to victims to push them toward scam phone numbers. Some YouTube creators have lost access to their channels.
  • Supply chain / third-party risk: Low to medium. Because the emails really come from Google, they get past normal filters, so staff should be warned not to call numbers in security alerts.
  • Source: X news summary
