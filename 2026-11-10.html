Digest for Nov 10, 2026

Daily internet & cloud health digest, Nov 10, 2026 (noon CT) Source: Scout run 2026-11-10-1030.md, plus direct checks of Cleo, OCI and Azure.

1. Cyber incidents

  • AhsayCBS backup server (Industry: Technology/backup providers; Actor: Unknown; Reported Nov 10; Status: Confirmed, actively exploited). An auth bypass and RCE (CVE-2026-105133/105134) has been used since early October to drop webshells and miners. Patches are out in 10.3.4.45 and 9.15.2.70. Supply chain risk: MSPs and hosts running Ahsay can expose their customers' backups. Source
  • Xen Orchestra (Industry: Technology/virtualization; Actor: Unknown; Reported Nov 10; Status: Confirmed, actively exploited). A REST-cookie auth bypass allows admin takeover and possibly root on XCP-ng hosts. Fixed in 6.10.0 and 6.9.1. Supply chain risk: hosting and MSP environments managing many tenants. Source
  • Qubole-linked AWS IAM role abuse (Industry: Technology/data platforms; Actor: Unknown; Reported Nov 9; Status: Investigating). Wiz and Mandiant report cross-account role abuse that drained customer cloud environments Oct 14-26. Supply chain risk: any customer that trusted Qubole's cross-account roles. Source
  • Chrome zero-day CVE-2026-125350 (Industry: Technology; Actor: Unknown; Reported Nov 10; Status: Confirmed, exploited, patched in 157.0.8103.50). It isn't in CISA's KEV catalog yet. Source
  • Breach disclosures (Status: Confirmed): Frontline Education (3.09M people, education tech), Revolve (1.5M, retail), Amplifon Americas (1.3M, healthcare). Third-party risk is mainly for the affected individuals and the schools Frontline serves. Source

3. Internet, network, cloud and EDI

  • Spectrum user reports have been above baseline since about 7:20 AM CT, scattered cities, with no confirmed outage and nothing Iowa-specific. Ongoing, minor. Source
  • AT&T 5G Home Internet, mobile and broadband complaints today, with no official cause. Unconfirmed. Source
  • Cleo, OCI (Normal Performance) and Azure (no active events) are clear. No AWS, GCP or Cloudflare platform outage found.
